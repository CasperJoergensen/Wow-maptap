# Pinpoint Azeroth

A daily map game for **WoW Forever**, inspired by [MapTap](https://maptap.gg). Every day you get five places, from big cities to hidden corners, and tap where you think each one is on a label-free map of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms. The closer you are, the more points you score, up to 1,000.

- **Daily:** the same five places for everyone, once per day (rolls over at the daily reset).
- **Practice:** unlimited random games.
- **History & stats:** replay any past daily on the map, plus streaks, averages and a score histogram.
- **Share:** copy a result line like `Pinpoint Azeroth #12 — 873/1000 🎯🎯🔥🙂😬`.

**Commands:** `/pa` (or `/pinpoint`) opens the game; `/pa help` lists the rest.

**Install:** copy this repository into `Interface/AddOns/PinpointAzeroth`.

**Retail (for testing until Forever launches):** the addon also loads in retail. Zones that Cataclysm reshaped (the Barrens, Stranglethorn, Hillsbrad and Alterac, Badlands, Dun Morogh, Eastern Plaguelands) are left out there, so retail plays with 79 of the 100 places, and its dailies differ from Forever's.

Not in this MVP yet: the guild board and the options panel (see [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)).
